# Critical Care Master Reference — Content Gap Analysis

## Method and boundary

This coverage audit compares the present repository against the clinical domains specified in the Master Reference brief. A page title is not counted as adequate coverage: the domain must contain physiology, diagnostic reasoning, management principles, nursing surveillance, complications, uncertainty, claim-level evidence, and appropriate cross-links. `Present` therefore means only that a starting page exists.

Official-source checks performed on 2026-08-12 confirmed important moving targets: SSC adult sepsis guidelines 2026, AHA CPR/ECC 2025, J-SSCG 2024, Japanese critical-care nutrition guidance published in 2025, ESICM circulatory shock/hemodynamic monitoring 2025, and a **public-review draft—not final guidance—** for KDIGO AKI/AKD 2026. These must be distinguished by publication status.

## Cross-cutting missing infrastructure

1. **Claim-level citations:** important thresholds and recommendations are not linked to exact source passages, population, recommendation strength, and exceptions.
2. **Evidence labels:** established physiology, guideline recommendation, evidence suggests, common practice, expert opinion, and uncertainty are not consistently separated.
3. **Threshold registry:** MAP, oxygenation, plateau pressure, driving pressure, ICP/CPP, hemoglobin and glucose values need source/context/exception metadata.
4. **Terminology authority:** a repository-wide Japanese/English abbreviation and preferred-term guide is missing.
5. **Contradiction detection:** sibling pages are not yet compared at claim level for duplicate or inconsistent statements.
6. **Human sign-off:** high-risk content lacks named specialty review and local-policy verification.
7. **Figure provenance:** all figures need evidence-match narratives; generated raster anatomy should be replaced with traceable, medically reviewed illustration or programmatic diagrams where possible.
