# Infection Control / Procedures Quiz

## Q1

安全な注射practiceは？

A. syringeのみ交換しneedle再使用  B. one needle/one syringe/one patient/one time  C. single-dose vial共有  D. sink横で調製

**Answer: B.** Standard Precautionsの基本。  
**Knowledge:** [Precautions](../17_Infection_Control/Precautions/STANDARD_TRANSMISSION_PRECAUTIONS.md)

## Q2

CAUTI予防で最も重要なlifecycleは？

A. routine culture  B. indication–aseptic insertion–maintenance–daily removal review  C. odorでantibiotic  D. 定期交換のみ

**Answer: B.** device necessityとclosed maintenanceが中心。  
**Knowledge:** [Device Infection](../17_Infection_Control/Device_Infection_Prevention/DEVICE_INFECTION_PREVENTION.md)

## Q3

procedure timeoutに含めるべきものは？

A. siteだけ  B. patient/site/procedure/roles/risk/rescue/specimen  C. operator名だけ  D. 終了後のみ

**Answer: B.** critical stepsとrescueまで共有する。  
**Knowledge:** [Procedure Safety](../18_Procedures/Safety_Framework/PROCEDURAL_SAFETY_FRAMEWORK.md)
