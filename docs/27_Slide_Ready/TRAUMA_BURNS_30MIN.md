# Trauma / Burns — 30-minute Slide Ready

## Learning Objectives

1. `<C>ABCDE`を介入後に反復できる。
2. 出血制御とdamage-control resuscitationを並行できる。
3. 熱傷の気道、TBSA、輸液、転送のpitfallを説明できる。

## Suggested Slide Structure

1. Trauma care as a repeated loop
2. Preparation and structured handover
3. Catastrophic hemorrhage and hidden compartments
4. Airway/breathing immediate threats
5. Disability and TBI SSOT
6. CT vs OR/IR decision
7. Lethal diamond
8. MTP activation and stop points
9. Pelvic/thoracic pitfalls
10. Burn ABCDE and inhalation injury
11. TBSA/depth estimation
12. Formula as starting point, not autopilot
13. Circumferential/chemical/electrical burns
14. Occult hemorrhage case
15. Take-home messages
