# Cardiac Critical Care — 30-minute Slide Ready

## Learning Objectives

1. ACSと機械的合併症をtime-criticalに認識する。
2. heart failure/cardiogenic shockをcongestion–perfusionで捉える。
3. 不安定arrhythmiaとROSC後careの再評価loopを説明する。

## Suggested Slide Structure

1. Cardiac ICU threats; 2. ACS serial ECG; 3. Reperfusion activation; 4. ACS complications; 5. Congestion–perfusion; 6. Cardiogenic shock escalation; 7. MCS: phenotype and exit; 8. Pulse/instability first; 9. Tachycardia; 10. Bradycardia/pacing; 11. High-quality arrest care; 12. ROSC oxygen/BP; 13. Cause/coronary pathway; 14. Temperature/seizure; 15. Delayed prognosis and survivorship.
