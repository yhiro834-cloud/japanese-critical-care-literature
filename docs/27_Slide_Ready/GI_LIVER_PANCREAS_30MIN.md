# GI / Liver / Pancreas — 30-minute Slide Ready

## Learning Objectives

1. GI bleedをstabilization–localization–hemostasisで整理する。
2. ALF/HEの転送・神経・凝固pitfallを説明する。
3. pancreatitisとIAH/ACSのfluid/source-control判断を行う。

## Suggested Slide Structure

1. GI bleed ABC; 2. Hb/risk pitfalls; 3. Variceal vs nonvariceal; 4. Endoscopy/IR/surgery; 5. Rebleeding; 6. ALF definition; 7. Etiology and NAC; 8. Transplant call; 9. HE/cerebral risk; 10. Coagulation; 11. Pancreatitis diagnosis; 12. Moderate fluid/nutrition; 13. Necrosis; 14. IAH measurement; 15. ACS decompression.
